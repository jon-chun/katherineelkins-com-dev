Katherine Elkins's book-length work spans computational narrative, the philosophy of mind, and AI — and the long inquiry into embodied experience that connects them.

Cambridge University Press, 2022

The Shapes of Stories: Sentiment Analysis for Narrative

Can computational methods reveal something about literary narrative that close reading alone cannot? The Shapes of Stories introduces SentimentArcs — a methodology for tracking emotional arc across full-length texts — and applies it to canonical works from Austen to Kafka. The open-source framework has been downloaded over 95,000 times from more than 4,000 institutions in 198 countries, and its methods have been adopted across novels, film, political speech, medical narratives, and social media.

Oxford University Press, 2022 — Editor

Proust's In Search of Lost Time: Philosophical Perspectives

An interdisciplinary collection examining Proust's novel through the lens of philosophy of mind, memory, consciousness, time, and embodied experience. Contributors include leading philosophers and literary scholars. Elkins also contributes a chapter, "Proust's Consciousness."
